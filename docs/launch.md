# Launch

The code is the easy part. An empty board is dead, so the first week matters most.

## Before sharing
Remove sample data. Post 5 real problems yourself from different people's mouths: ask five friends and write down what they say.

## Week 1 goal
30 real problems and at least one problem with 20 upvotes.

## Steps
1. Ask 10 friends and colleagues to post one real frustration they face with software, apps, or websites. Watch one of them use it on their phone and note where they hesitate.
2. Share a message in builder, student, and tech communities. Example: "Made a page where we drop what frustrates us about technology so builders can discover what is worth solving. Takes one minute, no login needed: [link]."
3. Share on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, tech subreddits, or developer WhatsApp/Discord communities.
4. After 3 days, post the top 3 problems back to the groups and ask people if they relate (upvote).
5. Tell people about the Contributors list: reach 10 upvotes and your name and photo go on the site.

## Weekly habit
Read the board every Sunday. Pick the problem with the most upvotes that nobody solves well today. Talk to three people who tapped it. Build the smallest fix. Mark it Building, then Launched, and thank the Contributor by name.

## Watch for
- Posts that attack named people or staff: hide them and keep the board about problems, not people.
- Many posts about one topic: that is your signal.
- Few upvotes on everything: the problems are too vague. Ask posters to be specific.
